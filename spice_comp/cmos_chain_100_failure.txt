100-inverter chain transient, 1 us stop, 62.5 ps maximum step.
Matched Level-1 benchmark, ordinary operating-point initialization.
Original Amber fails before transient begins: LinearSolveError: Newton matrix is singular at iteration 3.
Stack trace confirms _initial_transient_state -> operating_point -> _continuation_step -> _newton.
No timing result or waveform comparison is claimed for this case.

Source inspection: _continuation_step calls _newton directly and only handles a
returned nonconverged flag. LinearSolveError escapes before continuation step
subdivision or pseudo-transient fallback. This identifies an error-handling gap;
the underlying numerical singularity/conditioning cause was not established.
The chain was not seeded with different initial conditions to obtain timings.

Recovery implemented after investigation: treating an intermediate linear-solve
error as a rejected continuation step lets the existing pseudo-transient fallback
converge the 100-stage operating point without altered initial states. The probe
reports converged=true and alternating 5/0 V output rails. Regression additionally
checks the full DC residual and a genuinely floating source still failing.
The 100-stage transient waveform has not been validated and has no timing claim.
